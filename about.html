HEAVY MUSIC,SOFT PAPER.

DROP D is a listening zine for nu metal and everything tuned down: the records that came out of bedrooms and basements and somehow ended up on the radio between pop songs.

it looks like a fashion magazine on purpose. the heaviest music was always made by the softest people, so we print it on the softest paper we could find. it's pixels. it's still soft.

there are no reviews and no stars. every song has a real deck under it. you don't read about a record here, you put it on.

tuned
to D

the name

WHAT IS
DROP D?

a guitar normally has six strings tuned E A D G B E. drop d means you turn the lowest one down a whole step, from E to D. the guitar gets heavier and darker, and you can play a power chord with one finger. that's half of nu metal right there.

try it: turn the peg, then pluck the string.

noteE82.4 hz

standard tuning
