the crate 11

this is a listening zine for music tuned a whole step down. we print it on the softest paper we could find, because the heaviest records were always made by the softest people.

heavy

HOME/heavy.jpg — a soft, pastel photo that only shows through the letters

nu metal was the sound of the kids who weren't invited. seven-string guitars, a dj in the corner, a singer who whispered and then didn't. in 2001 it was on the radio between pop songs, and nobody could explain why.

we don't rate records here. we put them on. every song in this issue has a real deck under it: start it, stop it, drag it backwards. switch it to 45 and listen to a whole genre turn into a cartoon.

drop d is the tuning: the lowest string down from e to d, one whole step. it's how a guitar sounds when it stops being polite. it's also how this page sounds. turn it up. the paper can take it.
